# Mystery ledger (private, not shown in the game)

Use this to plan slow reveals. Rule: each chapter answers one small question and raises another. Never reveal the antagonist's motive before the final region.

## The secret (final act only)
A powerful human woman (the Lamplighter) is working against the Gods because they killed her beloved. Draft thread so far: she begged for "one more night" at the Old Gate on the night of the Dim Vigil, the lantern went dark, and she stopped being afraid. The Hush is her tool or her consequence. You can decide which.

Open decisions for later:
- Whose body is the Descender wearing? (The player is an ordinary person from a normal world who woke inside a Descender, a being who falls between galaxies. Is the original owner gone, sleeping, or watching?)
- Is the Lamplighter the same "someone from your world" who scratched the engraving?
- Who is Aurel? (Beloved, most likely. Possibly a patient, possibly a healer.)
- Are the Nine real gods, or a story the world tells itself?
- Why does Pulse not remember the Dim Vigil?
- Why does the Hush speak to the Descender?

Twist idea on the table: gods treat illness as fate, and healing is heresy. The Descender and the Lamplighter are both healers, one who failed and one who is still trying. The ending asks what the Descender does with that mirror.

## Clue map, Region 1 (Neurology)

| Chapter | Clue planted | Question it raises |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue | The Hush speaks in chapter 1; a bell in the opening line | Why does it speak? What bell? |
| 1. Ferryman | Moonbells at every dark lantern | Who leaves them? |
| 2. Courier | The Hour Bell: everyone hears it before they fall, but no bell exists | Where is the bell? |
| 3. Keeper | The Dim Vigil: a woman begged all night, the Old Gate lantern died | Who was she? |

| 5. Silent Hall | Engraving in non-Lumaran handwriting: a star and "I will light them all". The Hush: "She never finished either." | Another Descender? Who is "she"? |
| 6. Weaver | Tapestry: the Nine, backs turned on a kneeling figure | What did the Nine do? |
| 7. Messenger | Pip's letters to Aurel at the Old Gate, never answered | Who writes them? |
| 8. Two Winters | The Dark Map: dark lanterns form a path | Where does it lead? |
| 9. Broken Bridge | The bridge was cut by hand, cleanly | Who helps the Hush? |
| 10. Mistwood | First sighting of the Lamplighter: dark cloak, long red scarf, leaves moonbells | Is she the one behind the Hush? |
| 11. Cartographer | The Dark Map is complete and ends at the Old Gate | What is behind the Gate? |
| 12. Old Gate | Hale's memory of the woman's plea; nine seals, one cracked | Which seal cracked, and why? |

Note: the Lamplighter's red scarf echoes the Descender's scarf. Decide if that is a coincidence in the story or the first sign that the two are mirrors.

## Region 2 onward
Each new region should add one new layer: a new place the Gods touched, a new face of the Hush, and one more piece of the beloved's story. Keep every patient's illness tied to the region's theme, and keep every clinical fact accurate.

## Patients are the clues
Each person the Descender treats carries a symptom-story that is also a world-story. Keep that rule and the mystery will feel earned.
